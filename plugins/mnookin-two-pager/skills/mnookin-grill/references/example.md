# Calibration example

Reference for `mnookin-grill`. Before grilling, have one finished two-pager in
front of you to calibrate the target altitude: **concrete, ~two pages,
values-based — not aspirational fluff.**

## Why this is a fabricated example, not a link to a real one

A two-pager is a personal career document. Pinning a named individual's
two-pager into this skill — even one they self-published — is a bad trade: the
reference rots the moment they edit or remove it, and it isn't ours to keep
permanent. The invented example below does the calibration job with nothing to
rot and no one's private information in it.

If you want a real-world reference, the *Never Search Alone* community does
publish example two-pagers from time to time — search for a current one. Don't
hardcode it back into this file.

## What "good" looks like (the bar to grill toward)

- Fits ~two pages; every section concrete, not aspirational.
- Deal-breakers name the actual situation that would make the person leave —
  not "bad culture."
- Must-haves name the axes explicitly: work mode / geography, sector, role
  scope, management style, comp floor.
- The weakness section is a real trade-off, not a disguised strength.
- Goals distinguish the 12–18 month move from the 3–5 year direction; the
  long-term one may be less settled, as long as it is *honestly* unsettled.

## Fabricated example — "Sam Rivera" (a composite, not a real person)

Placeholder tokens like `<metro>` / `<floor>` are left blank on purpose — this
is a shape to match, not a profile to copy.

### 1. What I love doing
Turning a fragile manual process into a system other people can rely on.
Debugging the thing nobody else wants to own. Writing the design doc that makes
a hard decision obvious. Mentoring one or two engineers closely.

### 2. What I hate / won't do
Coordinating status across five teams as the main job. Being the permanent
owner of a legacy service with no mandate to change it. Roles where
"architecture" means slide decks and no code.

### 3. Must-haves
Remote, or hybrid within ~40 min of `<metro>`. Senior IC track with a real
staff path — not "lead = manage." A team that ships to production weekly. Base
at or above `<floor>`. A manager who has done the work and will argue with me.

### 4. Must-nots
On-call for systems I don't get to fix. A "we'll sort out levelling later"
offer. Sole engineer on a project with a hard customer deadline. More than
~25% client-facing.

### 5. Strengths
People hand me the ambiguous, half-broken problem because I will define it and
then close it. Written proposals that change decisions. Test infrastructure
others adopt and keep running after I move on.

### 6. Weaknesses / development areas
I under-invest in visibility — good work goes unseen because I don't socialise
it. I stay in implementation detail past the point where delegating would serve
the team better. Speaking to large rooms.

### 7. Short-term goal (12–18 months)
Senior/staff platform or infra role where I own a system end to end and set the
testing bar for it.

### 8. Long-term goal (3–5 years)
Staff-level technical leadership across a domain — direction and standards, not
headcount. Domain still open: leaning developer-productivity or ML
infrastructure, not settled.

### 9. Ideal company
Series B–D, 150–800 people, technical founders still close to the product. A
sector with a real-world constraint (health, energy, logistics) over pure
consumer. A culture that writes things down and disagrees in the open.
