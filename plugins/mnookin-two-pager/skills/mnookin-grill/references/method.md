# Mnookin Two-Pager — Method

Reference for the `mnookin-grill` skill. This is the generic exercise; the user
keeps their own working draft and career notes wherever their config file points.

From Phyl Terry's *Never Search Alone*, named after Allison H. Mnookin. A
strictly two-page career-clarity document, pressure-tested through a "listening
tour" of conversations, then locked and used as a filter for opportunities.

## Phase 1 — Build the draft (solo)

Hard limit: two pages. Nine sections:

1. **What I love doing** — the work I'd do unpaid; energizing tasks
2. **What I hate / won't do** — drains, deal-breakers, past regrets
3. **Must-haves** — non-negotiables (comp floor, work mode, role scope, team,
   mission, tech)
4. **Must-nots** — things that would make me quit within a year
5. **Strengths** — what colleagues consistently rely on me for
6. **Weaknesses / development areas** — honest, not humble-brags
7. **Short-term goal** — next role, 12–18 months
8. **Long-term goal** — 3–5 years
9. **Ideal company** — stage, size, domain, culture

Draft from existing career facts / evidence notes. Don't polish — honest and
complete first. `mnookin-grill` runs this phase as a one-question-at-a-time
interrogation.

### Empty skeleton (create the two-pager from this if it doesn't exist)

```markdown
# Two-Pager — <name>

## 1. What I love doing

## 2. What I hate / won't do

## 3. Must-haves

## 4. Must-nots

## 5. Strengths

## 6. Weaknesses / development areas

## 7. Short-term goal (12–18 months)

## 8. Long-term goal (3–5 years)

## 9. Ideal company

## Open questions for the listening tour
```

## Phase 2 — Listening tour

6–10 conversations, ~45–60 min each, with:

- former managers / skip-levels who saw the work directly
- 2–3 peers who worked closely with the person
- 2–3 people who know the target market well
- ideally 1–2 who will disagree

Format each call:
1. 30–40 min: catch up; ask their read on strengths/market *before* showing the
   doc (avoid anchoring)
2. Last 15 min: share the two-pager, ask:
   - Where does this not match how you see me?
   - What's missing or overstated?
   - What roles/companies does this point you to?
   - Who else should I talk to?
3. Log notes same day: date, person, their read, surprises, revisions triggered.

Optional: form / join a *Never Search Alone* search group (5–6 peers) to run
this in parallel rather than alone.

## Phase 3 — Revise & lock

- Revise after every 2–3 conversations
- Stop when 3+ consecutive conversations produce no material change — convergence
- Locked version becomes the filter: score shortlisted and new opportunities
  against must-haves / must-nots before applying

## Checklist template

- [ ] Phase 1 draft written
- [ ] Contact list for listening tour (target ~8)
- [ ] Outreach sent
- [ ] Conversations held (___ / 8)
- [ ] Revision pass 1
- [ ] Revision pass 2
- [ ] Converged / locked
- [ ] Shortlist re-scored against locked criteria

## If the listening tour won't happen

Common. The draft then stays a self-portrait — no outside correction on whether
the strengths are how others experience the person, or whether an "I saw it
early" claim is insight or self-flattery. Partial substitutes, weaker but real:

- Market-research questions (is a target title / comp band realistic) →
  answerable from job postings and posted bands, not people.
- Strengths / timing reality-checks → test against written artifacts already on
  hand: past performance reviews, peer feedback, separation documents.
- Lead-fit screening → convert into interview questions that detect a
  peer-challenge vs. deference lead.
